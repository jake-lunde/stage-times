## Notes

- Three stages, as printed in the column heads: CAMP, FLOG, GNAW. Ids `camp`, `flog`,
  `gnaw`; names Camp Stage, Flog Stage, Gnaw Stage.
- Every set prints a start and an end. Nothing is guessed (`end_inferred: false` throughout).
- Poster casing is kept exactly as printed (all caps); casing is display only.
- "EARL SWEATSHIRT + MIKE" and "THE ALCHEMIST & FRIENDS" are one set each, as printed.
- The colors are the posters' own three column colors: cream for Camp, orange for Flog,
  blue for Gnaw.
- Card art: the festival's own share image (its CAMP FLOG GNAW CARNIVAL lockup), unchanged.
