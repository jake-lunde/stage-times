# Transcription log — Corona Capital 2026

Read by hand off the festival's three day posters (Viernes 20, Sábado 21, Domingo 22), each
read again at full resolution, then checked set by set against the posters by a second reader.

Provenance: the festival's Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/coronacapital/) posted the set
times stage by stage on 2026-11-12 (the Viva Tent post, for one, shows the same times as the
Viernes poster). The three day posters went out to the Mexican press the same day; the copies
here are the 2160×2700 originals as carried by Sopitas (2026-11-12), which credits them to
OCESA, the festival's promoter. Each carries the CORONA CAPITAL lockup, the day, and the OCESA
and ticketmaster.com.mx marks. Fetched 2026-11-13. The festival's own site had no set times.

Source images: `bd37bd02638d4c458417947ce9e95bfab89c5d2fb4dbd3893f2209e68e76e0c9.jpg` (Viernes 20),
`c51602927b7d7c08e7c525f4a1fa1ffc546670cd5529f6e55db83ffdedd85a11.jpg` (Sábado 21),
`f22f05de9c154a192a30a5ab7e38e4322d125fb56bb51ef130a4c69cf4cd7739.jpg` (Domingo 22).

Press text lists of these times disagree with the posters in places (Jamie xx, Tricky, Johnny
Marr among them). Only the posters were used.

## Corrections made on review

None — a second reader (another model) read all three posters on its own first, then matched
all 73 sets against this file: no discrepancies. It noted that ΣTELLA's first letter is a
sigma-like glyph (the artist styles the name with a Greek Σ) and that The Strokes' time is
printed without spaces; both are kept as printed.

## Notes

- Five stages, as printed in the column heads: CORONA, DORITOS, CORONA CERO, NIVEA, VIVA TENT.
  Ids `corona`, `doritos`, `corona-cero`, `nivea`, `viva-tent`; names Corona, Doritos,
  Corona Cero, NIVEA (as the festival writes it), Viva Tent. The stages are named for their
  sponsors, so a later year may rename one; each year has its own URLs, so that is fine.
- Every set prints a start and an end. Nothing is guessed (`end_inferred: false` throughout).
- No set starts after midnight. Seven end after it (Gorillaz, Jamie xx, Twenty One Pilots,
  Sebastien Tellier, Rikas, The Strokes, Santigold); their end moves to the next calendar date
  and they stay on the night they started.
- Poster casing is kept exactly as printed (all caps); casing is display only. Special
  characters as printed: ΣTELLA (a Greek capital sigma), BØRNS, MS*GLOOM, MODEL/ACTRIZ,
  CÉLINE DESSBERG. "BUNT. (IN THE ROUND)" is one set, as printed.
- Colors: the posters print only three column inks for five stages. Corona (navy), Doritos
  (lime) and NIVEA (blue) keep theirs; Corona Cero and Viva Tent take two other blues from
  the festival's own art.
- Card art: the festival's own Mexico City relief map and CORONA CAPITAL lockup, the two
  layers its site's lineup poster is built from (poster_bg.webp and logo-corona-capital.png on
  coronacapital.com.mx), set together as the lineup poster sets them, lineup text left off, cut
  to 6:5.
