Stage Times

Stage art, drawn from the set times

Five generative families, seeded the way the build already seeds its capsules, with the real Capitol Hill Block Party 2026 sets driving every parameter. Pick a family, a ground, and Glow, and look at the four stages side by side.

What each family is, and what drives it

Rays Flat

A shape per stage, half the art height, and one ray per set leaving its edge. The rays are a clock: 2 PM at twelve, a set\u2019s start time is its position, its length is its width, so a stage that runs late fills the bottom of the dial. Auto assigns circle, triangle, square, octagon, kite, hexagon, diamond, pentagon by stage order, frozen like the color. Point (v1) is the earlier single-source version.

Ridgeline Flat

One line per day, front to back. Each set is a hump on the line: position is start time, height is length, guessed ends get a softer peak. The Unknown Pleasures gesture, but every peak is a real set.

Rings Flat

The stage as a record. One ring per set, in running order from the center out; stroke weight is set length. The center drifts with the seed so no two stages sit the same way.

Noise field Seeded only

Lines displaced by seeded value noise, the Book of Shapes landscape. Amplitude follows set density. It is the one family that is texture rather than information; here for comparison.

Capsules Shipping

What the stage cards draw today. Same seed, same integer math.

What stays true in every family

  • Seeded by festival-key/stage-id through the same FNV-1a and mulberry32 the build uses. The variant slider appends a digit to the seed so you can see the range; the build would pick one and freeze it.
  • Integer coordinates, flat fills, opacity tints of cream and a deepened cut of the stage color. No gradients, no filters, no blur. Halftone is real circles in a pattern tile, which is how a screenprint gets a glow.
  • Every parameter that carries meaning comes from the sets: count, start time, length, guessed end, how late the stage runs. Nothing from the artist names except the seed, because a name is not a sound.
  • The headliner names still have to sit on top at 18px semibold. Toggle them to check legibility per family; rays and rings are the ones that fight the text.
  • Drift is CSS only, a four-minute rotation, off under reduced motion. The build output stays byte-identical; the page moves.

The ruling: art slots may glow, nothing else may

Flat-only is lifted for the art area of a card and for nothing outside it. Grounds, pills, chips, type, and card edges stay a single-step color change. Inside the art slot, gradients and bloom are allowed, which is what Glow turns on: a radial falloff from the light source and a blurred copy of the composition underneath it. Halftone stays as the screenprint alternative for anyone who wants the glow without the gradient.

  • Deep ground plus Glow is the neon reference. Stage color stays the hue, so a red stage is still red; the ground is the stage cut toward black, not a generic dark.
  • Bloom is an SVG blur filter. It costs paint time on a phone; four cards is fine, a carousel of twelve should render the bloom into the committed SVG at build rather than filter live. Worth measuring before it ships.
  • The headliner text still has to clear contrast on top of a glow. Check it with the Deep ground: cream on a lit wedge drops below 4.5:1 near the source, so the names sit low in the slot and the source sits high, or the names get a flat cream tab behind them.
  • The skill needs one line added under Non-negotiables 3: "Card art areas are exempt: gradients and blur are allowed inside the art slot only."
